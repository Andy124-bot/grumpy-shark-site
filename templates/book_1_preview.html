About This Book

In *Grumpy Shark's First Adventure*, our cranky hero lets out some awful scary grumbles, The reason for his Grumpyness is that he hasnt eaten for a long time because of a bad tooth. but soon discovers that with the help ofothers, he can find a way to overcome his troubles and make new friends along the way.
